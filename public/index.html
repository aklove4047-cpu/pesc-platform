About Purvanchal Education & Skill Council (PESC)

Purvanchal Education & Skill Council (PESC) is an autonomous council established with the visionary goal of fostering vocational, technical, computer, and skill-based educational excellence across rural and urban territories of India.

Council Objectives

To standardize vocational training, bridge industry skill gaps, provide standardized syllabi, and deliver universally verifiable certifications for students.

Quality Standards & Transparency

100% digital governance with real-time QR code verification, audited marksheet evaluations, automated student portals, and regional coordinator monitoring.
