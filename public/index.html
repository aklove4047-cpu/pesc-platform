About Purvanchal Education & Skill Council (PESC)

Purvanchal Education & Skill Council (PESC) is an autonomous national educational organization established with the visionary mission of fostering vocational, technical, computer, and skill-based educational excellence across India.

Autonomous Statutory Framework

Registered under the Indian Public Trust Act 1882, working in adherence to the National Education Policy guidelines for skill-based vocational literacy.

ISO 9001:2015 Certified Quality Governance

100% digital governance with real-time QR code verification, audited marksheet evaluations, automated student portals, and regional coordinator oversight.
