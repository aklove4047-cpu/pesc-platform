FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Answers to Common Questions

Everything you need to know about centre affiliation, courses, certification validity, and examination procedures

Are PESC vocational and computer certificates valid across India? +
Yes. Purvanchal Education & Skill Council (PESC) is an autonomous national organization registered under the Indian Public Trust Act 1882 and ISO 9001:2015 certified. Certificates are widely accepted in the private sector, corporate hiring, self-employment, and skill eligibility requirements across India. Every certificate carries a public QR code that verifies instantaneously.
How can employers or institutions verify student marksheets online? +
Employers can instantly verify credentials by scanning the QR code on the physical certificate/marksheet with any smartphone camera, or by entering the Certificate Number or Student Roll Number in the Verification Portal right on our homepage.
What are the minimum infrastructure requirements to start a franchise centre? +
The applicant centre must have a minimum of 3 to 5 working computers/laptops, a high-speed internet connection, a printer/scanner, an office desk for candidate inquiries, and a qualified instructor having at least DCA/ADCA/BCA or equivalent background.
How long does the affiliation approval process take? +
Once you submit your application online, our central verification team inspects the submitted details within 24 to 48 hours. Upon approval, your centre code, official Affiliation Certificate, and automated subdomain website (e.g. yourcentre.pescouncil.in) are provisioned immediately.
How does the centre examination and marksheet dispatch system work? +
Centres conduct theory and practical tests following PESC council syllabus guidelines. Centre directors enter student scores directly into their Franchise Portal. Once verified by the council controller, digital marksheets and certificates are issued immediately, and physical certificates can be downloaded or dispatched.
Can students access their Admit Card and ID Card online? +
Yes. Every enrolled student receives dedicated login credentials to the Student Panel (`/student`), where they can view their study centre details, download their Examination Admit Card / Hall Ticket, view course syllabus, print digital ID cards, and access their official Statement of Marks.

About Purvanchal Education & Skill Council (PESC)

Purvanchal Education & Skill Council (PESC) is an autonomous national organization established with the visionary mission of fostering computer, vocational, technical, and skill-based educational excellence across India. We bridge the gap between rural and urban educational opportunities by enabling grassroots computer institutes with cutting-edge academic curricula and verifiable digital certification.
